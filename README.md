I would love to further my understanding in automatic testing and further explore failures and how to properly read through and understand them better. I really enjoyed working with embedded systems and would love to continue to almost pick them apart to better understand. I loved the flexibility of the Pi and how most embedded systems are made for one function or purpose, which we made our Pi into, as a temperature sensor.
